The business challenge

Having information is not the same as having control.

Financial systems, contracts, spreadsheets and operational tools all contain valuable information. But when these operate in isolation, management can struggle to see the complete picture.

Reports may explain what happened without revealing why. Cost pressures can remain hidden. Important renewals and obligations can be missed. Technology can create more administration instead of better decisions.

Disconnected information

Data sits across systems, spreadsheets, documents and teams.

Limited visibility

Financial and commercial risks are difficult to connect to operational performance.

Unclear next steps

Analysis does not always translate into ownership, action and follow-through.

Legal, regulatory & compliance support

Make your obligations visible. Put practical governance around them.

South African businesses operate across company law, tax administration, employment, information protection, consumer obligations and industry-specific rules. The exact requirements depend on the entity, its activities, size, people and operating locations. StratForge helps management organise the business-side processes, records and oversight needed to stay on top of applicable obligations.

▤

Corporate governance & statutory administration

Support the organisation of company records, director and shareholder information, governance calendars, annual-return tracking, beneficial-ownership filing reminders and board or management action registers. Formal statutory filings and legal interpretation remain with the relevant company and its appointed professionals.

⌑

Contract & commercial obligation management

Build contract registers and obligation schedules covering key dates, renewals, notice periods, service levels, pricing reviews, deliverables, counterparties and document ownership. Highlight upcoming decisions and escalation points before deadlines are missed.

▣

Regulatory compliance mapping

Help identify the regulatory themes relevant to the business and translate them into an obligation register, responsible owners, evidence requirements, review frequency and management reporting. This may include tax administration, labour, health and safety, consumer, privacy and sector-specific requirements.

◎

POPIA & information governance coordination

Assist with practical information-management processes: personal-information inventories, access and retention registers, incident escalation workflows, policy administration and evidence tracking. This is operational support, not a legal POPIA assessment or a substitute for the organisation's Information Officer and specialist advice.

✓

Risk, controls & compliance monitoring

Document key business risks and controls, map control owners, schedule periodic checks, record exceptions and track remediation. Provide management dashboards that show overdue actions, emerging exposures and evidence gaps.

↗

Due diligence & transaction readiness

Organise financial, commercial and governance information for funding discussions, supplier onboarding, customer reviews, tenders or potential transactions. Identify missing records and unresolved actions for review by the appropriate professional advisers.

A practical compliance workflow

From scattered obligations to accountable action.

We help build a repeatable management process rather than simply handing over a list of legislation.

01 · Map. Understand the business model, entity structure, workforce, information flows, customers, suppliers and locations.

02 · Assign. Record obligations, due dates, accountable owners, required evidence and escalation routes.

03 · Monitor. Introduce calendars, registers, periodic reviews and exception reporting.

04 · Escalate. Flag matters requiring interpretation, formal advice, certification or representation to appropriately qualified and authorised professionals.

Important legal boundary: StratForge is not a law firm and does not practise law. We do not draft or interpret legal opinions, advise on legal rights or liabilities, negotiate or litigate disputes as legal representatives, certify legal compliance, or provide legal representation. Where a matter requires legal advice or reserved legal services, the client must instruct an appropriately authorised independent legal practitioner. StratForge may support the business process and coordinate information at the client's direction.

Examples of South African frameworks that may be relevant, depending on the business, include the Companies Act, tax legislation administered by SARS, the Basic Conditions of Employment Act, Labour Relations Act, Occupational Health and Safety Act, Compensation for Occupational Injuries and Diseases Act, POPIA, PAIA, Consumer Protection Act and applicable sector or municipal requirements. This is an illustrative, non-exhaustive list — it is not a determination that every law applies to every client.
